# Boundaries

ArbiterSTG is non-governing. It is a diagnostic routing and classification layer.

## It Does Not Authorize

ArbiterSTG does not authorize:

- execution;
- meaning;
- truth;
- value;
- origin;
- inheritance;
- observer completion;
- legal liability;
- moral guilt;
- system safety;
- final accountability.

## Boundary Warnings

Do not treat trace absence as proof of non-execution.

Do not treat trace presence as proof of meaning.

Do not treat admission as validation.

Do not treat routing as truth.

Do not treat transfer as ownership.

Do not treat institutional assignment as origin.

Do not treat observer access as execution itself.

Do not treat proxy legibility as full residue recovery.

## Overclaim Pattern

An overclaim occurs when a trace condition is used to support a conclusion beyond its structural capacity.

Examples:

- A record is admitted, then treated as proof of truth.
- A proxy route is assigned, then treated as origin.
- A bridge residue is found, then treated as full recovery.
- A missing trace is treated as non-execution.
- A partial trace is used to close observer incompletion.
